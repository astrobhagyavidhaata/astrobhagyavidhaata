This Policy describes how Astro Bhagya Vidhata proposes to handle information when you use the website, mobile application, kundli tools, wallet, AI-assisted astrology and consultations. We will update this document if the actual product or applicable law changes. Do not launch features that collect or share data in a way not described here.

## 1. Information we may collect
Depending on the features you use, information may include:
- **Account and verification:** mobile number, OTP verification status, account ID and account security events. OTP codes should be used for verification and must not be stored as readable long-term account data.
- **Profile and birth details:** name, date of birth, time of birth, birthplace, gender if you choose to provide it, and chart preferences.
- **Consultation information:** questions, chat messages, session timestamps, duration, astrologer/service identity, and support or complaint correspondence.
- **Wallet and payment records:** recharge amount, transaction identifiers, payment status, wallet ledger entries, refunds and relevant billing records. Full card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN and banking passwords should not be collected or stored by this platform.
- **Technical and security information:** IP address, device/browser details, log data, approximate usage events, fraud signals and error reports, where necessary for security and service operation.
- **Support information:** details you provide when reporting a problem or requesting a refund.

Please do not submit another person's personal information unless you have an appropriate lawful basis and authority to do so.

## 2. Why we use information
We use information only for specified and legitimate service purposes, including:
- verifying your mobile number and maintaining your account;
- calculating and displaying the kundli and saving charts you request;
- providing consultations and maintaining conversation continuity;
- displaying wallet balance, processing payments, preventing duplicate charges and handling refunds;
- protecting the platform, detecting fraud, investigating complaints and enforcing our Terms;
- providing customer support and important service/security notifications;
- meeting accounting, tax, legal and regulatory obligations;
- improving reliability and product quality using proportionate diagnostics.

We will seek separate consent where required for optional marketing messages or any new purpose that is not reasonably connected to the service you requested. Refusing marketing consent must not block essential account or transaction messages.

## 3. AI processing and service providers
If you use an AI-assisted consultation, the relevant question, chart details and limited conversation context may be processed by technology service providers to generate and deliver the response. Hosting/database services, OTP/SMS delivery providers and payment processors may also process the information needed to provide their respective functions.

Before launch, the operator must:
- maintain an internal record of the actual providers and provide any provider disclosures or links required by applicable law;
- configure contracts, access controls and data-processing settings;
- review provider retention, model-training and international-transfer settings;
- send only the minimum information required for each request;
- never send payment secrets, OTP codes, passwords or unnecessary identity data to an AI model.

Do not promise that an AI provider never retains data unless the selected account, contract and settings have been verified to support that statement.

## 4. Sharing and disclosure
We do not sell personal information. We may disclose the minimum necessary information:
- to technology, hosting, database, OTP, payment and customer-support providers acting to provide the service;
- to professional advisers, auditors or insurers where reasonably necessary;
- to law-enforcement, regulators or courts when legally required or to address an immediate serious safety threat;
- in connection with a business reorganisation, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable law.

We do not make birth charts or private consultation conversations public by default. Access by authorised support personnel should be limited, logged and only for a defined support, security, legal or quality purpose.

## 5. Security
We use or plan to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including HTTPS, role-based access, database row-level security, server-side secrets, restricted administrative access, audit logs, backups and payment-provider signature verification. Wallet changes must be performed server-side and must not rely on values supplied by the browser.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your phone and OTP private, report suspected account misuse promptly, and avoid using shared devices for sensitive sessions. We will handle security incidents and notifications in accordance with applicable law.

## 6. Retention
We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including providing saved charts and chat history, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, and meeting legal, accounting and tax obligations. Different records may have different retention periods. Before launch, the operator must set and document actual retention periods for OTP/security logs, chats, charts, payment records and support tickets, and securely delete or anonymise information when it is no longer required, subject to legal holds.
